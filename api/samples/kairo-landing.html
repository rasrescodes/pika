FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes, really. Starter is free forever with no credit card required. Upgrade only when you need more projects, unlimited invoices or client portals.

Can I import my existing clients and projects?

Absolutely. Import from CSV, Toggl, Harvest, Trello or Notion in a couple of clicks. Most people are fully set up in under 10 minutes.

How do I get paid through Kairo?

Connect Stripe or PayPal and clients can pay invoices by card, Apple Pay or bank transfer straight from their portal. We charge zero extra fees on top of your processor.

Does Kairo work offline and on mobile?

Timers keep running offline and sync the moment you’re back online. Native iOS and Android apps plus a desktop tracker keep you covered everywhere.

Can I cancel anytime?

One click, no hoops. Cancel whenever you like and keep access until the end of your billing period. You can always export all of your data.
