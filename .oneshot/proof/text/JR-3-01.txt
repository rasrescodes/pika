METHOD

Held-out sites: picked by hand, never used for corrections. Jury scores copied from each site's Awwwards page (linked).
Each critique is one Juror call through Mubit: the same fixed instructions, a screenshot we took, no art director notes.
Speed: wall-clock time per call. Cost: token usage returned by the provider times the published price in data/pricing.json, mean per call, times 100.
Anything missing shows as unavailable. Nothing is estimated.
