multi-column-grid: three or more columns of cards, links or tiles.
- Measure one column's x and width, the gutter and the count; every card's x follows. Write out every card, never "repeat".
- Cards are rects (fill, radius, border) with MEDIA inside for images. List the card rect before its media.
- Card text repeats a style: one shared-style line per role (titles, captions, prices), an item per card.
- Rows cut off by the bottom of the frame still count: draw the visible part.
- A headline or filter row above the grid sets its top: measure the gap.
- Usual mistakes: a wrong column count, equal cards when widths differ, missing borders and radii.
