list-index: a text list of projects or links is the hero.
- The rows are the design. Measure the first row's y and the row pitch, then give every visible row its own item with exact copy.
- Columns within rows each need their own x; same-style columns share one line.
- Row dividers are 1px rects across the list width, one per row; they carry most of the edges.
- A highlighted row may have a fill or another colour: draw it.
- The type is small, so size errors are cheap but position errors are not.
- Usual mistakes: rows merged into one paragraph, missing dividers, a drifting row pitch so the bottom rows land far off.
