centred-hero: headline and call to action centred over a plain or media background.
- The Builder never centres by itself: give each line its own left x and fit width, or a box with centre and its width.
- Measure the vertical position; centred heroes often sit above or below the frame's middle.
- Buttons: a rect with fill, radius and border at exact size, the label centred in it.
- Background: a flat colour, a CSS gradient with real stops, or a full-size media box.
- The nav is usually a thin top row: one shared-style line with an x per item.
- Usual mistakes: assuming x=720 without measuring, a wrong gradient direction, buttons without their fill.
