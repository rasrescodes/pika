big-type-poster: one or two huge words or lines carry the hero.
- The display type is most of the score. Give every display line its own item with x, y, size and fit to its ink width.
- Size from cap height: poster type often runs 150 to 450px and fills the width edge to edge.
- Line height is usually tight, 0.8 to 1.0 x size: measure baseline to baseline.
- Condensed faces are common: choose a condensed font or heavier weight, never a wide font squeezed thin.
- Bands and marquees near the type are large colour fields: draw them.
- Usual mistakes: type too small, lines merged into one item, a missing bottom band.
