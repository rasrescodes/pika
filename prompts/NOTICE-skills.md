# Third-party notices: generator-skills-v1

`generator-skills-v1.md` is a condensed derivative written for Pika. It restates, in our own words and cut down to a single static 1280 x 720 hero, guidance from the three open-source design skills below. No long passages are copied. Each source keeps its own licence; the full licence texts live in the linked repositories.

- Repository: https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable
